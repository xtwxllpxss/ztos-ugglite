## Publication rules respected

- No `build/`, firmware, images, binaries, keys, credentials, MAC/SSID/IP,
  serials, host names, partition paths or raw logs added.
- No third-party Windows Phone screenshots; only facts and links to the original
  Microsoft pages.
- No foreign/demo APK (the ATL demo ran on the host build machine only).
- Every claim is labelled accepted / installed-and-verified / candidate /
  rejected; nothing built-but-not-run is presented as working.
- Links point only to files present in this repository; external links are
  sources, not mirrored assets.

## Excluded on purpose

- The WP reference contact sheet and its 12 frames (third-party assets), the
  private `build/` evidence tree, screenshots of our own before/after frames,
  device identifiers and logs. The prose states what the frames show; the frames
  themselves stay private.
