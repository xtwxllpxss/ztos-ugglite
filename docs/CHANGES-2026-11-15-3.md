## Publication rules respected

- No `build/`, firmware, images, APKs, binaries, package archives, keys,
  credentials, MAC/SSID/IP, serials, boot ids, host names or personal paths added.
  Partition identity is described only as "the extra filesystems".
- No device identifiers in the new texts; the only addresses mentioned are
  protocol-level (`0.0.0.0:2222`, `/dev/iio:device*`, `/dev/video*`) and public
  upstream paths.
- Every claim carries a status label; nothing built-but-not-run is presented as a
  feature. The notifications path, the camera builds and the software renderer
  are explicitly *not* claimed as accepted.
- Links point only at files present in this repository; the one external project
  link is named as work in progress and is not a dependency.

## Excluded on purpose

- The ATL/API packages, `api-impl.jar`, APKs (including the upstream demo and the
  tested games), per-run logs, frames and screenshots.
- The factory's queue files, generator and deploy scripts (they carry private
  demand lists and a device transport); only the method and the numbers are
  published. The generic form is a separate repository.
- `ztnotifyd`/`ztmetro` notification source delta (out-of-tree compositor line,
  same reason as the other compositor deltas).
- Boot health units and logs (partition identity), the wipe/install scripts and
  anything that could be replayed to touch the device.
- Raw Firefox traces and the A/B runner scripts.
